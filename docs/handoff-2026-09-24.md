## Read this before proposing nesting work

**The "concave NFP gap" does not exist.** clipper's `MinkowskiSum` is exact
for concave parts — measured twice, most recently at 12.1% tighter than the
convex hull's NFP on a deep L. A stale comment claiming otherwise has already
cost two separate sessions: one built and then deleted a whole convex
decomposition on the strength of it (commit `9ff78a9`), and one proposed it
again as priority work. `test/nfp.test.js` now guards the real behaviour.

The real limitation is the greedy first-fit search — a concave L places
exactly as many pieces as its convex hull despite the hull being 32% larger.
That is "stage two" in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-21-packing-density-design.md`, deliberately
deferred until exotic skins are actually being cut.

## Open, in rough priority order

1. **Nothing has ever been cut on a laser.** The machine is still undecided
   (80W Red Sail vs 150W Boss, both at a makerspace, neither used). Every
   clearance number in the repo is an assumption and kerf is not modelled at
   all. This is the largest gap against priority 1.
2. **Colour-threshold test** — agreed but never run. Needs Andrew to add
   several real hide pairs through the Hides page and say which he would call
   a match and which he would reject, including one genuine close call. Until
   that produces a number, there is no line between "same colour" and
   "different colour", only a ranking.
3. **Multi-hide colour pooling for products** — Andrew confirmed he wants it:
   non-sneaker products should be able to draw from all hides of the same
   colour. Blocked on #2 for the threshold, and needs multi-bin nesting in
   `place.js`, which today nests onto one sheet only.
4. **Questions for Magna** are written up but unsent — seven questions and
   five material asks, covering whether a pair needs two hides or a full set,
   where "close enough" stops, and whether Magna shoots their own photos.
5. **The finish model is wrong.** The charts show cut (belly / tail / leg /
   hornback / full quill), treatment (suede / hand-painted / nappa), grade
   (gnarly), origin, and grain (pebble) — while the app models only
   `species` + `finish` (glossy/matte/semi-gloss), and "semi-gloss" appears
   in no supplier chart at all. Cut matters for matching specifically: a
   caiman tail and a caiman belly will never pair.

## Smaller open items

- The swatch chart **images** are not in the repo — they arrived as chat
  attachments and cannot be written to disk from there. To sample LAB per
  patch, save the originals into `docs/reference/swatches/` first.
- The charts carry **no scale reference**, so they are colour names only. One
  number from the supplier would unlock them: the physical size of a swatch
  patch on the sample card.
- **Finish is sometimes inside the colour name** ("Brown" and "Brown Suede"
  on the same chart; "Matte Black"; "Silver Metallic"). A colour vocabulary
  built on these strings alone would treat those as unrelated colours.
- **"Natural" is not a colour** — it is undyed, and a Natural cobra looks
  nothing like a Natural viper. Any name-to-colour lookup must key on
  species + name, never name alone.
- Hide sorting does not persist across page reloads.
- **Archive vs delete for hides**: decided to add archive when the first hide
  is actually used up, not before. Nothing is near spent yet.
